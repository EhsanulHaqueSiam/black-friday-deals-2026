About BF26 deals

Updated November 23, 2026

BF26 deals is an open-source directory of 2,826 live Black Friday and Cyber Monday 2026 deals on software, AI tools, VPNs, hosting, courses, games and hardware. The code and data are public on GitHub.

WHERE DEALS COME FROM

28 community deal lists on GitHub, plus web research: vendor sale pages, dated 2026 roundups and posts by the vendors themselves on X and Reddit. Companies can also submit their own deal; a maintainer approves each one. Every deal page links its sources.

HOW THE LIST IS KEPT ACCURATE

  • The site is rebuilt every day. Deals past their end date, links that no longer load and "coming soon" placeholders are removed.
  • Duplicates across lists are merged into one deal; vendor pages and hand-checked entries win over list rows.
  • Each page shows the date its deal last changed. Prices and codes come from vendors and deal lists, so check the price at checkout.
  • Anyone can report a deal that ended or a code that failed.

HOW DEALS ARE RANKED

Editors' picks and household brands come first. After them, deals are ranked by what visitors do (likes, shares, "Get deal" clicks, searches, views) and by how many independent lists carry the deal. "Best" answers on this site use that same order.

LINKS

Vendor links go straight to the vendor. Some are copied from the source lists and may carry those lists' referral or tracking parameters.
